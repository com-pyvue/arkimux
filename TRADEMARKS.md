# ArkiMux name and logos

You can use the ArkiMux name and logo without asking permission to talk about
the project, publish a tutorial, organize a meetup, or show that your product
works with ArkiMux. For example, “Acme Connector for ArkiMux” is welcome.

Make clear who publishes your product or runs your event. Don’t describe a
third-party release, fork, or service as official, certified, or endorsed by
ArkiMux when it isn’t. “Official ArkiMux Connector” would give the wrong
impression for an independent connector.

This guidance covers the ArkiMux name and logo, not third-party brands. It
does not claim trademark registration or change your rights to use, modify,
or distribute the code under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE).
