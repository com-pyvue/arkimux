# ArkiMux name and logos

Draft proposal for owner review, prepared 5 November 2026. The owner of the
ArkiMux name and logos must be confirmed. This draft does not claim trademark
registration or ownership by an unconfirmed legal entity.

The code licence and branding are separate. [Apache-2.0](LICENSE) does not itself
grant rights to use the licensor's marks beyond the uses described in that
licence. Nothing in this draft limits other rights you already have, such as
applicable descriptive use, or adds a restriction to the code licence.

## Proposed branding rules

Allow accurate text references that identify ArkiMux as a dependency or describe
compatibility. Make clear who made and operates your product. Do not present a
fork, modified distribution or third-party service as an official ArkiMux release,
partnership, certification or endorsement.

Proposed permission process: ask the confirmed rights holder before using the
logo as your product's branding or implying a commercial relationship. Do not
claim that the logo is available under the source licence merely because the
repository displays it. No broader logo licence is granted by this draft.

A future brand policy must not be used to withdraw Apache code permissions or
forbid using, modifying or distributing code within those permissions. Any
separately supplied third-party marks remain subject to their owners' rights.
